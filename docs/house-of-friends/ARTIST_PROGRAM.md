# Artist Program

## Program Position

House of Friends is a selective artist-development program built around collaboration. It is not marketed as a battle, contest, or winner-take-all opportunity.

## Founding Class

Three emerging artists will be selected to form the House of Friends Founding Class of 2026.

### Core Experience

These items should be funded and guaranteed by the program before applications open:

- Featured collaborative B3B performance at Chasing Sun(Sets) II.
- Professionally filmed three-camera set.
- Direct master audio recording.
- Official release through approved Chasing Sun(Sets) media channels.
- Professional photography and short-form social assets.
- Founding Class identity and recognition.

### Partner-Supported Package

Only publish specific items after written partner confirmation:

- DJ equipment, headphones, USB drives, and artist merchandise.
- DJ software memberships.
- Educational and production-course memberships.
- Hospitality, festival passes, and venue credits.
- Additional filmed sessions or development resources.

### Future Opportunities

Use `consideration for` until dates and budgets are contractually confirmed:

- Joezi x Massuma afterparty.
- Kashmir programming.
- Hideaway programming.
- Future Chasing Sun(Sets), Untold Story, and Monolith opportunities.

## Applicant Experience

The application fee remains a working proposal. Before collecting payment, publish a clear statement of guaranteed value for every applicant.

Potential applicant value includes:

- Official House of Friends credential or lanyard.
- Chasing Sun(Sets) merchandise package.
- Educational digital session or resource library.
- Opt-in inclusion in the House of Friends artist network.
- Consideration for future collaborative programming.

Merchandise alone is not enough to justify the program. Applicants should receive a useful developmental or community benefit regardless of Founding Class selection.

## Selection Process

### Required Before Applications Open

- Eligibility rules.
- Geographic and age requirements.
- Submission requirements.
- Published application dates.
- Named or clearly described review panel.
- Scorecard and weighting.
- Conflict-of-interest policy.
- Selection and notification timeline.
- Terms for fees, refunds, withdrawal, and disqualification.

### Suggested Scorecard

| Criterion                           | Suggested weight |
| ----------------------------------- | ---------------: |
| Musical identity and curation       |              25% |
| Collaborative instinct              |              25% |
| Technical readiness                 |              20% |
| Community alignment                 |              15% |
| Professional intent and reliability |              15% |

Follower count should not be a primary selection criterion.

## Language System

Use:

- Founding Class
- Selected artists
- Artist pathway
- Collaborative sets
- Development program
- Community pipeline

Avoid:

- Winner
- Loser
- Battle
- Prize competition
- Pay to play
