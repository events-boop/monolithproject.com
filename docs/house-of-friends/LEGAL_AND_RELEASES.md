# Legal and Release Requirements

This document is an operational checklist, not legal advice. Final terms should be reviewed by qualified counsel.

## Application Terms

- Eligibility and age requirements.
- Submission ownership and permitted use.
- Selection process and discretion.
- Accuracy of submitted information.
- Fee, tax, refund, and chargeback terms.
- Withdrawal and disqualification.
- No guarantee of selection, booking, employment, or representation.
- Applicant communication and marketing consent.
- Privacy, retention, and deletion practices.

## Artist Agreements

- Performance services and compensation.
- Call times, technical obligations, and conduct.
- Name, image, likeness, and biography permissions.
- Audio and video recording rights.
- Editing, excerpting, distribution, and promotional usage.
- Artist review or approval window, if any.
- Release schedule and takedown process.
- Music licensing and track-clearance responsibility.
- Future booking language that avoids unintended guarantees.

## Content and Music Rights

- Determine whether full recorded DJ sets can be published on each platform.
- Establish copyright claim, mute, block, and takedown procedures.
- Retain cue sheets and artist-provided track information when possible.
- Separate master recording ownership from underlying composition rights.
- Define partner branding rights within artist content.
- Preserve unbranded masters where contracts require flexible reuse.

## Event and Venue

- Venue filming and photography permissions.
- Audience notice and release signage.
- Minor policy and age restrictions.
- Weather, cancellation, force majeure, and make-goods.
- Insurance, vendor requirements, and equipment liability.
- Drone and restricted-area permissions.

## Partner Agreements

- Cash and in-kind value.
- Category exclusivity.
- Product safety and responsible service requirements.
- Logo and trademark usage.
- Artist endorsement limitations.
- Content usage period and territory.
- Measurement methodology.
- Cancellation and replacement benefits.

## Publication Gate

Do not open paid applications until application terms, privacy language, artist releases, and a clear applicant-value statement are approved and accessible from the application flow.
