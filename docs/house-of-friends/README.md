# House of Friends

Working knowledge base for the House of Friends artist-development platform.

**Document status:** Internal working draft<br />
**Last consolidated:** July 13, 2026<br />
**Launch context:** Chasing Sun(Sets) II, August 22, 2026, Castaways Beach Club, Chicago

## Core Definition

House of Friends is an artist-development platform created by The Monolith Project. It launches inside Chasing Sun(Sets) II before developing into a recurring program of collaborative performances, education, professional content, community, and future opportunities.

House of Friends is not a separate August 22 event and is not positioned as a DJ competition.

> We are not looking for a winner. We are building the first House of Friends.

## Current Decision Status

### Locked Direction

- House of Friends launches inside Chasing Sun(Sets) II on August 22, 2026.
- Castaways Beach Club is the launch venue.
- Enoo Napa is planned for 8:00 PM–10:00 PM.
- Three artists form the House of Friends Founding Class of 2026.
- The language is `Founding Class` and `selected artists`, not `winner`.
- The program is built around collaboration, professional content, education, tools, and continued community.
- House of Friends remains a distinct Monolith platform rather than a sub-brand of Untold Story.

### Working Proposals

- Full programming begins at 11:00 AM.
- Six pre-headliner blocks with ten-minute transitions.
- The Founding Class performs a featured late-afternoon B3B.
- A $50 application or registration fee paired with defined value for every applicant.
- Partner-supported equipment, education, software, hospitality, and future bookings.

### Must Be Confirmed Before Public Launch

- Application opening and closing dates.
- Eligibility, judging criteria, panel, and selection timeline.
- Registration fee, payment terms, refund policy, and guaranteed applicant value.
- Partner commitments and exact in-kind or cash contributions.
- Performance opportunities that can be guaranteed versus offered for consideration.
- Artist releases, recording rights, music clearances, privacy terms, and event waivers.
- Public clearance for lineup and headliner language.
