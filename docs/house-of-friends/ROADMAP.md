# Roadmap

## 2026 — Founding Class

- Establish mission, identity, and operating system.
- Launch applications with transparent criteria.
- Confirm Founding Partners.
- Produce the August 22 House of Friends program.
- Record and release the Founding Class performance.
- Publish artist stories and partner recap content.
- Evaluate applicant and artist experience.

## 2027 — Sessions

- Recurring House of Friends Sessions.
- Expanded artist cohorts or seasonal classes.
- Education and mentor programming with defined curricula.
- A recognizable recorded-set catalog.
- Repeat partner packages based on 2026 reporting.
- Select activations across multiple Monolith event worlds.

## 2028 — Platform Expansion

- House of Friends tour or partner-city sessions.
- Scholarships and equipment grants.
- Structured artist incubator.
- Annual showcase.
- Deeper label, distribution, academy, and technology relationships.
- Alumni network and returning artist roles.

## Long-Term Measures

- Qualified submissions and application completion rate.
- Artist satisfaction and repeat participation.
- Content releases, watch time, and catalog growth.
- Future bookings generated for participating artists.
- Partner renewal and expansion.
- Educational resources delivered and used.
- Community retention beyond the launch event.

Scale should follow proof. Do not announce tours, scholarships, or an incubator as guaranteed programs before the first launch has been delivered and reviewed.
