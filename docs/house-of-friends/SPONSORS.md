# Founding Partner Program

## Partnership Position

House of Friends should be presented as a professional artist-development platform with defined partner categories, commitments, deliverables, and reporting. It should not be framed as a request for donated prizes.

## Recommended Categories

| Category                     | Role                                                  |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Founding Presenting Partner  | Primary program support and highest visibility        |
| Official Equipment Partner   | Hardware, headphones, USBs, and performance tools     |
| Official Education Partner   | Courses, memberships, and professional resources      |
| Official Technology Partner  | DJ software, production tools, and platform access    |
| Official Hydration Partner   | Non-alcoholic beverage and artist support             |
| Official Hospitality Partner | Artist lounge, gifting, beverage, and VIP integration |
| Official Venue Partner       | Venue and on-site operational support                 |
| Official Content Partner     | Recording, photography, editing, and releases         |

`Founding Presenting Partner` is preferred to `Title Partner` during year one so House of Friends retains a clear name and ownership.

Targets are prospects, not confirmed partners.

## Required Deal Terms

- Cash and in-kind valuation.
- Category exclusivity and conflicts.
- Guaranteed deliverables.
- On-site footprint and venue approval.
- Logo, trademark, artist-likeness, and content usage rights.
- Product quantities and delivery deadlines.
- Social, YouTube, and recorded-set integration.
- Measurement and post-event reporting.
- Weather, cancellation, and make-good terms.
- 2026 partnership term and renewal rights.

## Founding Partner Invitation

### House of Friends — Invitation to Become a Founding Partner

Dear Partner,

We believe the strongest communities are built by creating opportunities for others.

The Monolith Project creates intentional music experiences centered on connection, culture, and community. Through that work, we have encountered many talented emerging artists searching for something increasingly difficult to find: a genuine pathway to grow.

On Saturday, August 22, 2026, we will introduce House of Friends during Chasing Sun(Sets) II at Castaways Beach Club in Chicago.

House of Friends is a new artist-development platform created to support emerging DJs and producers through meaningful performance opportunities, professional content, educational resources, industry tools, and lasting community.

We are not creating another DJ competition.

We are building a pathway.

Three emerging artists will be selected to form the inaugural House of Friends Founding Class of 2026. Their core experience will include:

- A featured collaborative performance at Chasing Sun(Sets) II.
- A professionally filmed three-camera live set.
- An official release through Chasing Sun(Sets) media channels.
- Professional photography and social content.
- Recognition as members of the inaugural Founding Class.
- Consideration for continued opportunities across The Monolith Project ecosystem.

With the support of our Founding Partners, we intend to expand that experience through artist equipment, software, educational memberships, professional resources, gifting, hospitality, and additional development opportunities.

House of Friends is designed to extend beyond the three selected artists. Applicants will have the opportunity to join a broader creative community built around collaboration, education, future programming, and continued connection.

To bring this initiative to life, we are inviting a select group of organizations to join us as Founding Partners.

Partnership opportunities may include:

- On-site branding and experiential activations.
- Artist equipment and gift packages.
- Digital and social media recognition.
- Recorded-set and YouTube integration.
- Event signage and promotional materials.
- Artist and VIP hospitality.
- Educational resources and professional memberships.
- Long-term association with a platform designed to grow annually.

Chasing Sun(Sets) II will expand into a full-day experience beginning at 11:00 AM. House of Friends programming will move through collaborative performances and a featured Founding Class set before the day builds toward sunset and Enoo Napa's headline performance from 8:00 PM to 10:00 PM.

Our vision reaches beyond August 22. House of Friends is intended to become a recurring platform that develops artists, releases professionally produced performances, creates year-round stories, and builds lasting relationships between artists, audiences, and partner organizations.

We would be honored to discuss how your organization could help launch the inaugural House of Friends program.

Thank you for considering this opportunity. We look forward to building something meaningful together.
