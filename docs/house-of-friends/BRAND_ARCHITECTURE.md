# Brand Architecture

## Parent and Worlds

```text
THE MONOLITH PROJECT
        │
 ┌──────┼───────────────┐
 │      │               │
Sun(Sets)        Untold Story       Future event worlds
 │      │               │
 └──────┴───────┬───────┘
                │
        HOUSE OF FRIENDS
                │
        Artist Development
                │
 Performance • Education • Content
 Community • Tools • Future Bookings
```

The Monolith Project is the parent platform. Chasing Sun(Sets), Untold Story, Kashmir, Hideaway, and future concepts are environments in which House of Friends artists may develop and perform.

House of Friends is connective tissue across those environments. It is not owned by one event series and should not sit beneath Untold Story.

## Launch Relationship

On August 22, House of Friends launches **within** Chasing Sun(Sets) II. This gives the new platform a real audience, venue, production system, and cultural context without pretending it is already a standalone event.

## Domain Direction

- `monolithproject.com/house-of-friends` — canonical parent-site route while the platform is launching.
- `houseoffriends.vip` — recommended campaign domain when secured.
- The campaign domain should route into the canonical House of Friends experience with attribution preserved.

## Verbal Identity

### Core Lines

- We are building a pathway.
- Create opportunities. Build community. Invest in artists.
- We are not looking for a winner. We are building the first House of Friends.
- A platform for performance, content, education, and what comes next.

### Voice

- Human rather than institutional.
- Selective without becoming exclusionary.
- Ambitious without over-promising.
- Artist-first without treating partners as logos.
- Precise about what is confirmed.

## Visual Direction

House of Friends should remain inside Monolith's dark cinematic architecture while gaining a distinct community signal:

- Graphite and warm black foundation.
- Chalk-ivory typography.
- Restrained sage or oxidized-green signal color.
- Auburn and sunset-gold traces connecting it to Monolith and Chasing Sun(Sets).
- Architectural plan, table, doorway, and shared-signal etchings.
- Large type only at deliberate title moments.
- Shared pill and text-action CTA system rather than page-specific button inventions.

The identity should feel like a house being drawn into existence—not a bright startup template or a DJ contest flyer.
