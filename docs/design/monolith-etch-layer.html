INCISION · 06
The visual rule

More etch than glyph

Etch is atmosphere and can cover large parts of the surface. Glyphs carry meaning, so they stay rare. This meter counts what is actually drawn on this page right now.

— etch paths— glyphs
  1. Edge etching. Faint vertical carving along section edges, mostly offscreen (left rail, this section's right edge).
  2. Oversized geometry. One huge composition, about a fifth visible, drifting slower than the content (hero).
  3. Incisions. Broken carved dividers; a short amber light travels through as you cross into the next section.
  4. Surface etching. Carving mapped onto the slab and the wall, inheriting their perspective.
  5. Activation. Hover, playback, navigation and live status run Etched → Revealed → Amber signal → Dormant.
  6. Semantic sequences. Page families carry their own hidden words.
