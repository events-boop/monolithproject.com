Is the event 21+, and what ID do I need?+

Yes. All guests must be 21 or older and bring valid government-issued photo identification. Admission is subject to security screening and venue entry policies.

Where do I buy or find my tickets?+

Purchase through the official AllEvents listing. Have the mobile QR ticket from your confirmation ready to scan on arrival. Check your selected ticket for any arrival-time requirement. If you cannot locate your confirmation, check your spam folder and contact the team with your booking details.

When will artist set times be available?+

JOEZI and MASSUMA are the announced headliners. Support lineup and performance times will be posted in the DJ set times section when confirmed. Artist order and performance times are subject to change.

Who can help with VIP, tables or groups?+

Check the official ticket listing for current VIP passes and inclusions. For table availability or group enquiries, email events@monolithproject.com before booking.

Weather, venue alerts & ticket policies

Is the event rain or shine?+

Yes. Chasing Sun(Sets) III is a rain-or-shine event. Rain alone does not mean the event is cancelled. Dress for an outdoor lakefront setting and check official updates before leaving for the show.

Could weather affect the schedule or venue?+

Rain or shine does not rule out changes needed for safety. Severe weather or unsafe conditions may affect entry, performance times, the venue or the event date. Confirmed changes will be announced on SUNSETS.VIP and Monolithproject.com.

Where will new venue and event alerts be posted?+

New venue alerts and confirmed event changes will be listed on SUNSETS.VIP and Monolithproject.com. Check these websites before travelling and again on show day. Email and SMS alerts will also be used as those channels become available; do not rely on receiving a message as your only source of updates.

Is postponement the same as full cancellation?+

No. A postponement moves the event to a later date; a full cancellation ends the event instead of rescheduling it. For example, rescheduling the show with JOEZI and MASSUMA is a postponement, not a full cancellation. A venue change also does not, by itself, mean the event has been cancelled. The official announcement will state the event status and next steps for ticket holders.

What happens to tickets if the show is postponed?+

The postponement announcement will confirm the rescheduled date when available, the artist details, how existing tickets will be handled and any applicable refund options or deadlines. Do not assume that a postponement has the same ticket terms as a full cancellation.

What is the refund policy if the event is fully cancelled?+

Ticket sales are final except as provided by the applicable ticket terms or a confirmed full-cancellation notice. If the event is fully cancelled, the official announcement will explain the refund process and timing. A postponement, venue change or weather-related adjustment should not be treated as a full cancellation. Review the terms attached to your booking; for a specific ticket question, contact events@monolithproject.com.

At the venue

Can I leave and re-enter?+

Confirm re-entry rules with the venue team before leaving; do not assume your ticket allows a second entry. For questions before show day, email events@monolithproject.com.

Will food and drinks be available?+

Food and cocktails will be available from Castaways. If a venue change is announced, check the updated event information for food and drink arrangements at the new location.

What can I bring?+

Bag, outside-food and other item restrictions follow the venue’s policies. Email events@monolithproject.com about a specific item before travelling, and check any new venue announcement for updated entry requirements.

How do I ask about accessibility or another event question?+

Email events@monolithproject.com for accessibility arrangements, entry questions or help planning your visit. If the venue changes, contact the team to reconfirm any arrangements you need.
