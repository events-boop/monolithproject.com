Sun(Sets) III — postponed due to weather.

Due to weather conditions, Sun(Sets) III with JOEZI × MASSUMA is postponed. The safety of our guests, artists and crew comes first. A new date is not yet confirmed.

We know you were looking forward to this show, and we share your disappointment. Thank you for your patience and understanding as we work through the next steps.

Ticket holders will be offered admission to the rescheduled show and/or access to one of our Untold Story events as the indoor season opens. The new date and instructions will be shared as soon as they are confirmed.

The artists are working together to make the rescheduled show possible. The date and artist participation will be confirmed in a future update.

Please visit SUNSETS.VIP or follow our social channels for updates. Thank you for being part of this community. We look forward to being back on the dance floor with you.

Weather, venue alerts & ticket policies

Is the event rain or shine?+

Yes. Chasing Sun(Sets) III is a rain-or-shine event. Rain alone does not mean the event is cancelled. Dress for an outdoor lakefront setting and check official updates before leaving for the show.

Could weather affect the schedule or venue?+

Rain or shine does not rule out changes needed for safety. Severe weather or unsafe conditions may affect entry, performance times, the venue or the event date. Confirmed changes will be announced on SUNSETS.VIP and Monolithproject.com.

Where will new venue and event alerts be posted?+

New venue alerts and confirmed event changes will be listed on SUNSETS.VIP and Monolithproject.com. Check these websites before travelling and again on show day. Email subscriptions are not a substitute for checking this page. SMS alerts are not currently offered here.

Is postponement the same as full cancellation?+

No. A postponement moves the event to a later date; a full cancellation ends the event instead of rescheduling it. A venue change also does not, by itself, mean the event has been cancelled. The official announcement will state the event status and next steps for ticket holders.

What happens to tickets if the show is postponed?+

Ticket holders will be offered admission to the rescheduled show and/or access to one of our Untold Story events as the indoor season opens. The new date and instructions will be shared as soon as they are confirmed. Keep your booking confirmation. Contact events@monolithproject.com with a booking question.

What is the rain-or-shine and refund policy?+

This event takes place rain or shine. Guest, artist and staff safety comes first; severe weather or unsafe conditions may require a pause, delay, reschedule or cancellation. All ticket sales are final. No refunds will be issued. Ticket holders will be offered admission to the rescheduled show and/or access to one of our Untold Story events as the indoor season opens. The new date and instructions will be shared as soon as they are confirmed. Updates and ticket-holder instructions will be shared through our official channels.

At the venue

Can I leave and re-enter?+

Confirm re-entry rules with the venue team before leaving; do not assume your ticket allows a second entry. For questions before show day, email events@monolithproject.com.

Will food and drinks be available?+

Food and cocktails will be available from Castaways. If a venue change is announced, check the updated event information for food and drink arrangements at the new location.

What can I bring?+

Bag, outside-food and other item restrictions follow the venue’s policies. Email events@monolithproject.com about a specific item before travelling, and check any new venue announcement for updated entry requirements.

How do I ask about accessibility or another event question?+

Email events@monolithproject.com for accessibility arrangements, entry questions or help planning your visit. If the venue changes, contact the team to reconfirm any arrangements you need.
