Weather, venue alerts & ticket policies

Is the event rain or shine?+

Yes. Chasing Sun(Sets) III is a rain-or-shine event. Rain alone does not mean the event is cancelled. Dress for an outdoor lakefront setting and check official updates before leaving for the show.

Could weather affect the schedule or venue?+

Rain or shine does not rule out changes needed for safety. Severe weather or unsafe conditions may affect entry, performance times, the venue or the event date. Confirmed changes will be announced on SUNSETS.VIP and Monolithproject.com.

Where will new venue and event alerts be posted?+

New venue alerts and confirmed event changes will be listed on SUNSETS.VIP and Monolithproject.com. Check these websites before travelling and again on show day. Email subscriptions are not a substitute for checking this page. SMS alerts are not currently offered here.

Is postponement the same as full cancellation?+

No. A postponement moves the event to a later date; a full cancellation ends the event instead of rescheduling it. A venue change also does not, by itself, mean the event has been cancelled. The official announcement will state the event status and next steps for ticket holders.

What happens to tickets if the show is postponed?+

If a date change is confirmed, check the official notice for ticket-holder instructions and review the terms attached to your booking. A change of date alone does not establish refund or transfer rights. Contact events@monolithproject.com with a booking question.

What is the refund policy if the event is fully cancelled?+

Refund eligibility and any deadlines depend on your booking terms and the approved event notice. A cancellation, date change, venue change and safety delay are different situations; do not assume they carry the same ticket arrangements. Review the terms attached to your booking; for a specific ticket question, contact events@monolithproject.com.

What can I bring?+

Bag, outside-food and other item restrictions follow the venue’s policies. Email events@monolithproject.com about a specific item before travelling, and check any new venue announcement for updated entry requirements.

How do I ask about accessibility or another event question?+

Email events@monolithproject.com for accessibility arrangements, entry questions or help planning your visit. If the venue changes, contact the team to reconfirm any arrangements you need.
