01 / OPEN RADIO

Every frequency
has a home.

Listen now to Chasing Sunsets mixes and selected Karma Karma Radio and Drumcode episodes. Discover the selectors, explore their sounds, and follow credits back to the original publishers.

The radio vision +

Playing today: on-demand sets from Chasing Sunsets, Karma Karma Radio, and Drumcode. A continuous SUNSETS station, live broadcasts, and more independent community frequencies are part of the vision ahead.

Great selectors deserve to be heard for their taste and craft—not just their follower counts. We want every discovery to lead back to the artist and the people who brought it to life.

02 / OPEN MAPS

Discover where
music lives.

A set can lead to a venue, a festival, a city, or an entire scene. Open Maps is being designed to help listeners follow those connections into the real world.

The maps vision +

Imagine starting with a Chicago recording and meeting the scene around it—or tracing a beachside session to the people and places that shaped its sound.

More than pins on a map: source-backed musical stories. Open Maps is in development; the visuals here are concepts, not a live location service.

03 / OPEN PLATFORM

Discovery
without walls.

Music knowledge should travel with its credits, not stop at one website. Open Platform is our plan for responsible ways to share attributed public music information across communities.

The platform vision +

Future possibilities include open interfaces, embeddable listening, attributed records, and responsible integrations. Public creator onboarding, identity verification, and Open Platform integration access are not available yet.

The principle is simple: give creators credit, respect their choices, and let communities retain their own identity as the connections between them grow.

THE PRINCIPLES BEHIND SUN(SETS) PROTOCOL05 / 05

Trust is
the infrastructure.

Trust is not a badge. It means crediting the people who made the music, showing where information came from, and making room to correct the record.

Attribution. Provenance. Accountability. Consent. Integrity. These commitments guide today’s editorial choices and the tools we develop next. Formal identity checks and membership services are still being designed.

01

Attribution

+

The people behind the music receive appropriate credit.

02

Provenance

+

Information can be traced to evidence and its source.

03

Accountability

+

Contributions and corrections can be reviewed, challenged, and responsibly recorded.

04

Consent

+

Creators, listeners, and organizations control their participation and private information.

05

Integrity

+

Editorial discovery is earned through relevance and human judgment, not secretly purchased ranking.
